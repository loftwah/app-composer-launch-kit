# Design

Operate mode: useful source controls on the left, contextual previews and actual package on the right. Inherit the approved Composer charcoal/ivory, local Space Grotesk and canonical #FC5B05 action treatment with charcoal action text. Light normal-text/focus accent is #C74400. App identity and editable sample colours remain independent.

The sample is Night Signal: a purple analogue radio and an inquisitive moth. Existing identities remain untouched. Social typography is native editable text laid out after illustration. Tiny icons are displayed at their actual pixel sizes; install masks are explicit. Public links have no underline; keyboard focus remains visible. Controls remain stable while processing, cancel invalidates work, errors offer recovery. Reduced motion preserves the whole task.
