          Optional artwork assistance
          

            Local rendering needs no model, account or key. Optional artwork
            generation belongs in a trusted local process with explicit
            enablement, a deliberate request, a selected provider key and
            limits. It never runs in this public editor. Keep your approved
            artwork unless you deliberately choose a replacement.
